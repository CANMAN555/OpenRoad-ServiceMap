GRAND ISLAND, NEBRASKA

Boss Shop in Grand Island, NE, provides experienced truck and tire repair services at the highest standards in the industry. We provide many truck and maintenance services, as well as a large variety of new and retread tire brands. With more than 70-years of service to the trucking industry, you can trust Boss Shops to meet all your needs and get you back on the road.

From simple tire repairs and replacements to complete computer diagnostics, repairs, inspections, and PM’s, you can count on the thousands of certified Boss Shop technicians across the country to minimize your down time and get you back on the road. With you, to haul and back™.
