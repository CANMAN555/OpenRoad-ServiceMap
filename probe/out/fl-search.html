Welcome to Freightliner’s Dealer Locator – your ultimate resource for finding trusted service locations conveniently located along your route or in proximity to your current location.

At Freightliner, we recognize that your success hinges on keeping your fleet moving efficiently. That's why we're dedicated to making your job easier and more profitable. With our expansive dealer network, maintaining your Freightliner truck has never been more convenient. Whether it's routine maintenance or unexpected repairs, trust in Freightliner to keep you on the road and ahead of the curve.

When you choose Freightliner, you're getting access to more than 700 service locations and the reliability of our dedicated support staff, from our factory-certified technicians to our toll-free hotline operators. Our job is to make your job easier and more profitable, and with our vast network of service locations, it's never been easier to keep your Freightliner on the road.
